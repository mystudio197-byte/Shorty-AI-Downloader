Delete your Shorty data

  1. Return to the dashboard and choose Disconnect under Facebook Pages to end the current server session.
  2. In your video library, choose Remove for each copy you want removed from this browser. Alternatively, use your browser settings to clear site data for the Shorty website. Clearing site data removes all locally stored Shorty videos and edits on that device.
  3. To revoke Meta authorization, open your Facebook settings and find Business integrations or Apps and websites. Select Shorty AI Downloader and remove its access.
  4. Downloaded files in your Downloads folder are separate copies. Remove those yourself if you no longer want them.

Backend sessions are stored only in memory and expire within one hour, or when the backend restarts. Disconnecting does not delete the original videos on Facebook. This version does not maintain a cloud video library.
